## My Favorite Series: *Avatar: The Last Airbender*

> "Water. Earth. Fire. Air." - the opening narration

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated fantasy series that follows **Aang**, a young Air Nomad who is the last of his people and the *Avatar*, the one person who can bend all four elements.

### Main characters

1. Aang - the Avatar and an airbender
2. Katara - a waterbender from the Southern Water Tribe
3. Sokka - a warrior and strategist
4. Toph Beifong - an earthbending master
5. Zuko - the exiled prince of the Fire Nation

### Why I like it

- Strong character growth, especially Zuko's arc
- Great story that balances humor and serious themes
- Memorable music and animation
- Each element has its own fighting style
